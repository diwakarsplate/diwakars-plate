How to read the plate

White rings mark fixed strikes (24,600–25,400), and the cyan ring tracks spot. The gold dashed boundary marks where a separately simulated, centrally heated metal plate reaches its fixed activation temperature. Strike and spot positions use a linear radius scale. The selected strike is highlighted in magenta. Brightness of the halo near the selected strike represents a normalized financial overlay, not metal temperature.

The option calculator uses a simplified European Black–Scholes–Merton model with continuously compounded interest rate and dividend yield, 365-day time convention, constant IV and no transaction costs. These are illustrative values, not live NSE quotes. Values at expiry are intrinsic payoffs; Greeks at exact expiry are not displayed.
