THE FRAMEWORK

How the Plate works

Strike rings stay fixed

Each ring marks a strike. Its distance from the spot ring determines the spot-based intrinsic value of the call or put.

The spot ring follows NIFTY

As the market or scenario spot moves, the ring crosses strikes and the contracts’ moneyness changes.

The thermal layer shows extrinsic value

UDP maps the model premium minus intrinsic value to a signed display quantity. The pricing model supplies the premium.
