The foundation

Three ideas. One intuitive picture.

01 · Fixed strike rings

Concentric rings mark individual NIFTY option strike prices. They remain fixed while the market moves.

02 · Moving spot ring

The cyan ring follows NIFTY spot. Its position helps explain when a call or put is in, at, or out of the money.

03 · Central heat and glow

The disc is hottest at its centre. Its gold activation boundary marks the incandescence threshold—not a direct measure of an option's premium.

Learn with the simulator

From intuition to the Greeks

Change NIFTY spot, strike, implied volatility, or time to expiry and observe theoretical call and put prices. Explore Delta, Gamma, Theta and Vega as separate financial sensitivities.

Important: The heated plate is an educational analogy. Premiums are calculated using a simplified mathematical options model. The physical temperature and the financial valuation overlay represent different concepts.

About this project

Diwakar's Plate

An evolving educational framework developed to make option pricing and risk sensitivities more accessible through a consistent visual metaphor. Future releases may add synchronized NIFTY option chains, interactive strategies, more animations, and a learning library.

Educational content only; not investment advice. This prototype uses simulated inputs and should not be used to execute trades or predict market returns.
